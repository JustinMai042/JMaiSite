Welcome to Cal Poly Campus Explorer
🏛 Getting Started
Select a building from the left sidebar to load its 3D Gaussian Splat. Use the search bar to find a specific building.
🎮 Navigation Controls
W A S D
Move forward, left, back, right
Q / E
Move up / down
I / K
Look up / down
J / L
Look left / right
U / O
Roll left / right
🎬 Guided Tour
Click ▶ Guided Tour in the header to start an automatic cinematic flythrough. Use the controls at the bottom to pause, skip forward, or go back. Click ⚙️ Settings to adjust the tour speed.
ℹ️ Building Info
Click the ‹ arrow on the right edge of the viewer to open the building information panel with facts, classes, and more.
🗺 Minimap
A minimap showing the building location appears in the top left of the viewer. Hover over it and click the expand icon to enlarge it.
